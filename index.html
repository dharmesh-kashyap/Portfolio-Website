002/ The pipeline. Scroll to walk it.

Extract

The inputs are never clean.

Boundary files for 670K+ Pan India villages. 2,500+ government scheme PDFs. 60+ mutual fund instruments, consolidated on a recurring pipeline into one schema. Extraction ships with validation checks, so problems surface at the door.

GeoJSON. PDF. Python. Validation.

Transform

Five surveys. One schema.

HCES, ASUSE, AIDIS, SAS and the Agriculture Census, across three rounds of NSO and MoSPI microdata. PySpark and Hadoop do the heavy lifting, Polars and DuckDB shape and validate, and BI tools read the long format output directly. A DDI dictionary of 298 harmonized variables keeps definitions steady from round to round.

PySpark. Hadoop. Polars. DuckDB. Parquet.

Load

Staging in. Facts out.

A PostgreSQL warehouse over 5.2M+ records for ICICI Prudential. Staging tables are promoted into dimension and fact tables through a multi step pipeline, with query optimization for analytics. The Data Processor app puts a Flask front door on it.

PostgreSQL. Flask. SQL. Dimensional modeling.

Serve

Numbers people can use.

KPI dashboards with drill downs. AIV Hub deployed on AWS hosted servers, from setup and configuration to monitoring. When a cross subnet routing failure appeared, the fix came with written network and firewall requirements.

Tableau. AIV Hub. AWS.

Data Processor

Flask. PostgreSQL. ICICI Prudential. 2025 to 2026.

Finance teams were uploading Excel files manually. This app takes Admin Dump and Location Vacation files into staging tables, validates them, and promotes them into the dimension and fact warehouse.

It grew a scheduler view, refresh progress, conflict reporting and a notification tray, and went through an audit that fixed duplicate promotion and staging filter bugs.
